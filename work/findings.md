# Findings

Only record findings with future value.

Good findings:
- recurring bugs
- architectural problems
- contradictions
- missing invariants
- process failures
- lessons likely to recur

Do not create findings for trivial imperfections.

## Template

### F-XXX — Short title

**Problem:**  
What is wrong.

**Impact:**  
Why it matters.

**Decision / Fix:**  
What should change or what changed.

**Related task:**  
T-XXX

**Status:**  
Open / Fixed
